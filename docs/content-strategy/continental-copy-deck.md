## Voice and terminology

The voice should be assured, specific and continental. It should name African institutions, people, systems and outcomes rather than relying on ceremonial language.

- **Continental:** Use for work spanning African institutions, countries or Agenda 2063. Do not use as decoration.
- **African:** Use when the subject is genuinely Africa-wide. Use Nigeria when the claim is Nigeria-specific.
- **Governance:** Institutional capacity, public policy, diplomacy, ethical leadership and state effectiveness.
- **Enterprise:** Institution-building, innovation, productivity, organisational growth and economic opportunity.
- **Education:** Educational leadership, human capital, teacher development and youth empowerment.
- **Transformation:** Pair with a concrete object or result. Avoid unsupported claims such as “transforming Africa.”
- Prefer “works with”, “convenes”, “advises”, “develops” and “builds” over grandiose verbs.
- Refer to planned Future Africa initiatives as planned or designed initiatives, not completed achievements.
