ARTSY VS. PHOTOGRAPHY — CREATIVE DIRECTION (STRAIGHT ANSWER)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

INSIGHTS (hub + articles)
Recommendation: ARTSY / EDITORIAL — not portraits
Why: Insights is a writing product. Text-led cards match high-end editorial sites. Individual articles may use optional hero art that is conceptual (typography, texture, abstract leadership/Africa motifs) — not another headshot. Use photography only when an essay is tied to a specific event or moment (e.g. a summit photo for a field note).

FUTURE AFRICA (/work/future-africa)
Recommendation: ARTSY / INSTITUTIONAL — not a Dr Akin studio portrait
Why: Future Africa is a continental platform, not a personal page. Best visuals: map/contour graphics, Agenda 2063 motifs, pan-African collaboration imagery, or documentary photography of summits and institutional settings — not a solo portrait.

AUCTUS AFRICA (/work/auctus-africa)
Recommendation: ARTSY / COMMUNITY PHOTOGRAPHY — not a Dr Akin hero portrait
Why: NGO/education brand. Warm illustration, community imagery (learners, teachers, classrooms — with consent), or abstract “growth/human capital” art. Dr Akin only if photographed at an actual Auctus programme.

WHERE PHOTOGRAPHY IS STRONGER THAN ARTSY
AALD, PERFORMX, and Erudio Hub are operating brands where Dr Akin is the visible founder. These benefit from real photography — speaking, consulting, training, education reform meetings — not pure abstract art. Artsy can support the hero, but human/institutional proof helps conversion.

KEYNOTE SPEAKING and PROFILE pages definitely need strong portrait and stage photography.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
TECHNICAL NOTES FOR PHOTOGRAPHER & RETOUCHER
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

ASPECT RATIOS THE SITE USES
• Hero portraits: approximately 4:5 vertical
• Event covers: 16:10
• Open Graph / social previews: 1.91:1 (1200×630)
• Book covers: approximately 2:3

DELIVERABLES
• WebP exports for web use
• High-resolution masters (TIFF or PNG) for archive
• Named files by page/use (e.g. hero-portrait-formal-v1.webp)

AVOID
• Heavy filters
• Busy backgrounds
• Using the same crop on every page

CONSENT & RIGHTS
• Any Auctus/community or audience shots require model/event releases
• AU or official institutional imagery requires usage rights confirmation before publishing
